The Philosophy of Quotidian

01

Deliberate Restraint

Inspired by Dieter Rams and Swiss editorial print, Quotidian removes every non-essential pixel. No confetti, no badges, no noise.

02

Tactile Analog Audio

Synthesized live with the Web Audio API, every checkmark produces an organic 2B graphite pencil stroke against paper.

03

Time-of-Day Cadence

Focus only on what is scheduled for right now—Morning, Afternoon, or Evening. Let the rest of the day wait until its time.

04

Private & Local-First

Your habits and daily reflection notes are stored in a local SQLite database. No tracking, no surveillance, full export to JSON or CSV anytime.
